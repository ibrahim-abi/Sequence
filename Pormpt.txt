3. CORE RULES & SEQUENCE INTERSECTION ENGINE
Deck Specification:

104 cards total (Two standard 52-card decks combined, no Jokers).

Cryptographically or Mersenne-Twister shuffled (random.SystemRandom() for true entropy).

Dead Card Rule:

During a player's turn, BEFORE playing a card, if they hold a non-Jack card whose both corresponding board coordinates (r1, c1) and (r2, c2) are already occupied by any chip, that card is marked DEAD.

The player may discard 1 dead card per turn, immediately draw a replacement card, and then proceed with their normal turn.

Jack Rules:

Two-Eyed Jacks (JC, JD): Wild Card. Place a team chip on any unoccupied space on the board.

One-Eyed Jacks (JH, JS): Anti-Wild Card. Remove any one opponent chip from the board, EXCEPT a chip that is already LOCKED in a completed 5-chip sequence.

76-Line Sliding Window Sequence Detection & Locking:

There are exactly 76 possible 5-in-a-row segments (windows of length 5) across the 10x10 grid:

10 rows × 6 horizontal windows = 60? Wait: in a 10-length line, a window of 5 slides 6 times (0..4, 1..5, 2..6, 3..7, 4..8, 5..9).

Horizontal: 10 rows × 6 = 60 windows.

Vertical: 10 cols × 6 = 60 windows.

Main Diagonals (NW to SE, length >= 5): 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 36 windows.

Anti-Diagonals (NE to SW, length >= 5): 36 windows.

Total = 192 distinct 5-cell windows on the 10x10 board.

Corner Rule: Corners (0,0), (0,9), (9,0), (9,9) act as permanently occupied by ALL teams simultaneously (only 4 team chips needed adjacent to a corner in a window).

Overlap & Locking Rule:

Once a 5-cell window is completed by a team, all non-corner coordinates in that window are added to locked_cells[team_id] and cannot be removed by a One-Eyed Jack.

A second sequence for the same team is valid if and only if it shares at most 1 non-corner cell (or 1 cell total) with any previously registered sequence of that team. (e.g., 6-in-a-row in the same direction shares 4 cells -> invalid as a 2nd sequence; 9-in-a-row in the same line sharing the center 5th chip -> valid as 2 sequences; two perpendicular lines crossing at 1 chip -> valid as 2 sequences).
